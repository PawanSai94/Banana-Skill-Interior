# Kitchen cabinet order — US standard cabinet codes

For quoting with Wayfair, RTA cabinet sites, Home Depot / Lowe's cabinet desks or a local cabinet shop.
Same layout and numbering as `floorplan.svg`, the wall elevations and `INVENTORY.md` (IKEA version).

**Standards assumed:** framed or frameless US cabinets · base 34½" H × 24" D (36" counter with a 1½" top) ·
wall cabinets 12" D · tall cabinets 24" D · code format = type + width + height (+ depth), e.g. W1818 = wall 18" W × 18" H.
Positions: east wall measured south from **A**, south wall west from **H**, west wall north from **P**.

## 6. Glass lift-up fronts — tell the supplier up front

US stock lines rarely sell horizontal lift-up or horizontal glass doors. Ask for one of:

1. Wall cabinets **"door-less / glass-ready"** (W1818, W2418) with **glass door inserts**, plus **Blum Aventos HK-XS
   lift systems** (≈ $100–150 each × 8) — keeps the lift-up look.
2. Standard **hinged glass doors** on the lower row (simplest, cheapest).

## 7. Not in this order (bought or built separately)

- Raised box with 3 toe-kick drawers (DIY) · pantry shelving (see `INVENTORY.md` §11)
- Countertop (25⅝"–26" D, ≈ 54 sq ft) · appliances (stovetop, hood, sink, DW, fridge, GE oven, microwave)

## Ask the supplier to confirm

1. A **30" oven cabinet (OC3090)** with oven + open coffee niche + microwave opening, or the closest option (33" needs 3" more width: borrow it from the appliance garage).
2. **BBC45** blind-corner pull distance (2½" filler on the east run, 3" on the south run).
3. Cooktop base **DB36-2** top-drawer height vs. your stovetop's depth below the counter.
4. Wall cabinet **18" heights** stacked 54–90" and the crown / scribe for the 96–100" ceiling.
5. Lead time, assembled vs RTA, and whether shipping is free above their order minimum.
