## Check before ordering

1. Is the counter end at **A** against a wall or open?
2. 18"-wide **lift-up** hinges/fronts and **glass horizontal** fronts in your door style (else glass hinged doors on U1, U4, U6).
3. Stovetop cut-out depth for cooktop base #3; GE oven cut-out for #13.
4. Rev-A-Shelf kit fits IKEA's frameless 18" (≈ 16½" inside).
5. Order #10 and the fridge fillers only after the fridge arrives.
