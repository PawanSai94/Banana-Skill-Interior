# Plywood box cut list (IKEA-compatible)

DIY boxes for the hybrid plan: **#1, #2, #5, #9** (drawer bases), **#7** (trash), **#8** (sink base),
**#11, #12** (tall appliance garage). Everything else comes from IKEA. Fronts come from a third-party front company.

The boxes copy **IKEA SEKTION** sizes so IKEA drawers (MAXIMERA), hinges (UTRUSTA), legs and the
pre-drilled third-party fronts fit them exactly.

## Key dimensions (match IKEA)

| | Base boxes | Tall boxes |
|---|---|---|
| Outside width (W) | 18, 24, 30, 36" | 24" |
| Height | **30"** (on IKEA legs → 36" counter) | **90"** (on the 9" raised box, no legs) |
| Depth | **24"** | **24"** |
| **Inside width** | **W − 1¼"** (same as IKEA, whose sides are 16 mm) | **22¾"** |

**The inside width is what matters.** MAXIMERA drawers are made for IKEA's inside width. Cut every bottom,
top, rail and shelf to the inside widths below, then use **⅝" plywood for the sides** — the outside width
lands within about 1/16" of IKEA's, which the fronts hide.

| Thickness | Use | Sheets (4 × 8 ft) |

| **Total** | | **10 sheets** |

Use cabinet-grade plywood (birch or maple, prefinished if you can). For the **sink base #8**, use a
moisture-resistant plywood or seal every edge.

## Joinery assumed

- Bottom, top/rails and shelves sit **between** the sides (butt joints; screws, dowels or confirmat screws + glue).
- Back sits in a **⅜"-wide × ¼"-deep rabbet** on the back edge of each side; bottom and rails stop in front of it.
- Base boxes: two 4" rails across the top (front and back) instead of a full top — the countertop covers them.
- One ¾" × 3½" **nailer** across the back (under the back rail) for screwing the box to the wall studs.

## Before drilling

1. **Buy one IKEA frame first** (for example cooktop base #3) and use one of its sides as a **drilling template**:
   copy the hole positions for the MAXIMERA runners, UTRUSTA hinge plates, legs and shelf pins onto every DIY side.
2. **Dry-fit one MAXIMERA drawer** in the first DIY box before building the rest.
3. Measure your actual plywood thickness — nominal ⅝" is often 19/32". Keep the **inside widths** above; the outside
   width will be up to 1/16" under IKEA's, which the fronts cover.
4. Seal the sink base (#8) bottom and cut edges with polyurethane or paint, and add a drip tray.
