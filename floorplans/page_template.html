Fixed decisions

  • 36" stovetop over a 36" two-drawer base, centred on the D–E wall (cook faces east), with a 15" oil-and-ladle pull-out beside it
  • 36" under-cabinet hood, vented straight out the east exterior wall
  • 33" sink in a 36" sink base, centred under the 32" window
  • 18" trash pull-out → sink → 18" cutlery drawers → 24" dishwasher (trash on the prep side)
  • 47" blind corner base at H, with a 26" × 26" glass-door corner wall cabinet above
  • Wall cabinets: glass lift-up fronts on the lower row, solid lift-ups above

West wall & pantry

  • French-door fridge, ≤ 36" wide and ≤ 76" tall, in a 41" alcove; 20" cabinet above
  • 48" appliance garage: two 24" × 24" × 90" tall cabinets with pull-out shelves
  • 30" tall cabinet: GE 28" convection oven, coffee counter at 38", microwave at 56"
  • Tall cabinets sit on a 9" raised box (flush, 24¾" deep) with three toe-kick drawers
  • Walk-in pantry, 36" × 61": U-shaped shelving, rolling rice and atta bins
